# Controls & Dynamics

Resources for classical, state-space, nonlinear, and optimal control, plus the optimization and dynamical-systems theory underneath them — the math that turns a robot's plan into stable, accurate motion.

## Resources

- **Classical Control Theory** — Brian Douglas
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUMWjy5jgHK1NC52DXXrriwihVrYZKqjk
  A free, beginner-friendly video series on transfer functions, block diagrams, root locus, and frequency-response (Bode/Nyquist) methods — the standard first pass at control theory.

- **Control Systems Engineering** — Benjamin Drew
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL5105727DD6E8DE98
  A free complementary video series covering the same classical control fundamentals (modeling, transient/steady-state response, root locus, frequency response, compensator design) with a more textbook-style, worked-example approach.

- **Control Bootcamp** — Steve Brunton, University of Washington
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMrJAkhIeNNR20Mz-VpzgfQs5zrYi085m
  A free video series on modern, state-space control: linear systems, controllability/observability, pole placement, LQR, Kalman filtering, and an introduction to nonlinear and data-driven control. A natural next step after classical control.

- **Nonlinear Control (Lectures on Nonlinear Systems)** — Jean-Jacques Slotine, MIT
  http://web.mit.edu/nsl/www/videos/lectures.html
  A free, 20-lecture graduate series on nonlinear control: Lyapunov stability, sliding mode and adaptive control, feedback linearization, and contraction analysis. Advanced/graduate level; pairs with Slotine & Li's *Applied Nonlinear Control*.

- **Convex Optimization I (EE364A)** — Stephen Boyd et al., Stanford University
  https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee364a/
  Covers convex sets, functions, and problems, plus least-squares, linear/quadratic/semidefinite programming, and interior-point methods — the optimization backbone of trajectory optimization and MPC. Lecture slides, notes, and the companion textbook (*Convex Optimization*, Boyd & Vandenberghe) are free; recorded lectures are restricted to enrolled students. Advanced-undergrad/graduate level.

- **Convex Optimization II (EE364B)** — Stephen Boyd et al., Stanford University
  https://web.stanford.edu/class/ee364b/
  The sequel to EE364A: subgradient/cutting-plane methods, decomposition, and advanced interior-point and stochastic optimization techniques. Same free-materials/gated-video pattern as EE364A. Graduate level.

- **Numerical Methods for Optimal Control** — Sebastien Gros, NTNU
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLc2vvxBHfBcrzR8fhWc7qjT1lr51Kjue2
  A free short-course video series on the numerical methods behind optimal control and MPC: direct/indirect methods, collocation, and NLP solvers as used for real-time trajectory optimization. Graduate level.

- **Optimal and Robust Control** — CTU in Prague (Automatic Control Laboratory, aa4cc)
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMLojHoA_QPmRiPotD_TnfdUkglTexuqm
  A free video lecture series covering LQR/LQG, H-infinity and robust control, and optimal control under model uncertainty. Advanced/graduate level.

- **Koopman Analysis** — Steve Brunton, University of Washington
  https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMrJAkhIeNNSVXUvppZTYNHKQUD-oWys9
  A free video series on Koopman operator theory — lifting nonlinear dynamics into a linear framework for analysis and control, an increasingly popular technique for data-driven modeling of robotic systems. Advanced.
